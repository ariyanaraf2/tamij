I turn complex problems into simple, well-engineered interfaces.

I'm a web developer with a focus on building products that are fast on day one and easy to extend two years later. My background is in JavaScript across the stack — React and Next.js on the front end, Node.js and PostgreSQL on the back end — with a habit of writing code that the next developer (often future-me) can actually read.

<FAQ />

Frequently asked questions

Everything clients usually ask before we start.

What's your typical project timeline?
Most single-page sites take 1–2 weeks; full web applications typically run 4–8 weeks depending on scope. I'll give you a firm estimate after a short discovery call.
Do you work with a fixed price or hourly?
Both — well-scoped projects get a fixed quote, and open-ended or maintenance work is billed hourly or as a monthly retainer.
Will I own the code and design?
Yes. Once the final invoice is paid, full source code and design assets transfer to you, including repository access.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Every project includes 30 days of bug-fix support after launch. Ongoing maintenance retainers are available after that.
Can you join an existing codebase mid-project?
Yes — I regularly join projects partway through. I'll start with a short code review so estimates account for the existing codebase.
